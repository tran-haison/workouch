## 4. Creating Workouts

Workouch offers two main ways to create workouts: **AI-Powered Generation** (Pro feature) and **Manual Creation** (Available in Basic plan).

### 4.1 AI-Powered Workout Generation (Pro Feature)

Workouch offers two AI workout generation modes:

#### The Shuffle Mode

Quick and easy workout generation:

1. Tap **"PRO"** or **"LAZY"** from the Home screen
2. Select **"The Shuffle"** mode
3. Describe what you need in a single sentence (e.g., "I want a full-body workout at home")
4. Tap **Generate** and wait a few seconds
5. Review the generated workout
6. Customize if needed, then save

**Tips for The Shuffle**:

- Be specific about your goals ("upper body strength", "cardio at home")
- Mention equipment you have available
- Note any injuries or limitations

#### The Neat Mode

Detailed customization for precise workout generation:

1. Tap **"PRO"** or **"LAZY"** from the Home screen
2. Select **"The Neat"** mode
3. Fill in all the details:
   - **Workout Name**: Give your workout a name
   - **Duration**: How long you want to work out
   - **Intensity**: Light, Moderate, or Intense
   - **Fitness Goals**: Strength, Endurance, Weight Loss, etc.
   - **Target Body Parts**: Select specific muscles/body parts
   - **Equipment**: What equipment you have available
   - **Location**: Home, Gym, or Outdoor
   - **Injuries/Limitations**: Any physical limitations to consider
4. Tap **Generate**
5. Review and save the workout

### 4.2 Manual Workout Creation

Create workouts manually with full control:

1. From the Home screen, tap **"PRO"** to go to the workout creation screen
2. Tap **"New Workout"** or the **+** button
3. Enter a workout name
4. Set rest time between exercises (optional)
5. Tap **"Select Exercises"** to browse the exercise library
6. Search or filter exercises by:
   - Muscle groups
   - Equipment
   - Exercise name
7. Add exercises to your workout
8. For each exercise, configure:
   - **Set Type**: Weight-based, Time-based, Distance-based, or Reps-only
   - **Sets**: Number of sets
   - **Reps/Weight/Duration/Distance**: Based on set type
   - **Rest Time**: Rest between sets (optional)
9. Reorder exercises by dragging (if available)
10. Save your workout

### 4.3 Set Types Explained

Workouch supports four types of sets:

- **Weight-Based**: Sets, reps, and weight (e.g., 3 sets × 10 reps × 50kg)
- **Time-Based**: Duration-based exercises (e.g., 60 seconds plank)
- **Distance-Based**: Distance-based exercises (e.g., 5km run)
- **Reps-Only**: Bodyweight exercises with sets and reps (e.g., 3 sets × 15 push-ups)

## 5. Executing Workouts

### 5.1 Starting a Workout

1. Go to your **Workouts** list
2. Select the workout you want to perform
3. Review the workout details
4. Tap **"Start Workout"** or **"Begin"**
5. You'll see a **Ready** screen - prepare yourself, then tap **"Start"**

### 5.2 During Your Workout

- **Current Exercise**: See the exercise name, GIF demonstration, and instructions
- **Sets Progress**: Track which set you're on
- **Timer**: View elapsed workout time
- **Rest Timer**: Automatic countdown between sets and exercises
- **Navigation**: Move to the next exercise or go back if needed

### 5.3 Completing Sets

For each set:

1. Perform the exercise as specified
2. Tap **"Complete Set"** or **"Next Set"** when finished
3. Rest timer will start automatically (if configured)
4. Continue until all sets are complete
5. Move to the next exercise

### 5.4 Finishing Your Workout

When you complete all exercises:

- You'll see a **Workout Summary** screen
- Review your total workout time
- Tap **"Finish"** to complete the workout
- Your workout history is automatically saved

## 10. Troubleshooting

### 10.1 App Crashes or Freezes

**Try these steps**:

1. Close and restart the app
2. Restart your device
3. Update to the latest app version
4. Check available storage space on your device
5. If issues persist, contact support

### 10.2 Data Not Syncing

**Check these**:

1. Ensure you're signed in to your account
2. Check your internet connection
3. Try logging out and back in
4. Force close and restart the app
5. Allow some time for sync (it may take a few moments)

**If data is missing**:

- Data syncs to the cloud automatically
- Check that you're using the correct account
- Contact support if data loss is suspected

### 10.3 AI Workout Generation Not Working

**For Pro subscribers**:

1. Verify your subscription is active (check Profile)
2. Ensure you have an internet connection (AI generation requires network)
3. Try again - generation can take 30-60 seconds
4. Check that you provided adequate input/preferences
5. Contact support if issues continue

**For Basic users**:

- AI workout generation is a Pro feature
- Upgrade to Pro to access this feature

### 10.4 Can't Sign In

**Troubleshooting steps**:

1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify your credentials (email/password)
3. Try "Forgot Password" if using email sign-in
4. For Google/Apple Sign-In, ensure those services are working
5. Try logging out and back in
6. Contact support if you're locked out

### 10.5 Workouts Not Saving

**Check these**:

1. Ensure you're signed in
2. Verify you tapped "Save" after creating/editing
3. Check your internet connection
4. Try creating a new workout to test
5. Contact support if workouts continue not saving

### 10.6 Exercise Library Not Loading

**Try these**:

1. Check your internet connection
2. Restart the app
3. Clear app cache (Android) or reinstall app (if needed)
4. Wait a few moments and try again
5. Contact support if the issue persists

### 10.7 Subscription Issues

See [Subscription Troubleshooting](#87-subscription-troubleshooting) section above.

### 10.8 General Performance Issues

**Optimize your experience**:

1. Keep the app updated to the latest version
2. Ensure sufficient storage space on your device
3. Close other apps to free up memory
4. Restart your device periodically
5. Check your internet connection for cloud features

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

### 11.1 General Questions

**Q: Is Workouch free?**
A: Workouch offers a free Basic plan with core features. AI workout generation requires a Pro subscription.

**Q: Do I need an internet connection?**
A: Internet is required for:

- Signing in
- AI workout generation (Pro feature)
- Syncing data across devices
- Browsing the exercise library

Once workouts are saved, you can view and execute them offline.

**Q: Can I use Workouch on multiple devices?**
A: Yes! Your account syncs across all your devices. Sign in on any device to access your workouts and profile.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures. Your data is protected with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. See our [Privacy Policy](https://vigorworkouch.com/privacy-policy) for details.

**Q: Can I export my workout data?**
A: You can view your workouts within the app. For data export requests, contact support at vigor.workouch@gmail.com.

### 11.2 Feature Questions

**Q: What's the difference between "The Shuffle" and "The Neat" modes?**
A: "The Shuffle" is quick and simple - just describe what you need. "The Neat" offers detailed customization with specific parameters like duration, intensity, goals, and equipment.

**Q: How accurate is the AI workout generation?**
A: AI-generated workouts are suggestions based on your preferences and profile. Always review and adjust workouts to match your fitness level and goals. Consult healthcare professionals for medical advice.

**Q: Can I customize AI-generated workouts?**
A: Yes! After generation, you can edit exercises, sets, reps, weights, and rest times before saving.

**Q: How many exercises are in the library?**
A: Workouch includes thousands of exercises with detailed information, GIF demonstrations, and instructions.

**Q: Can I create workouts without equipment?**
A: Yes! Filter exercises by "Bodyweight" or "No Equipment" to find exercises that require no equipment.

**Q: Are there workout templates?**
A: You can create and save your own workout templates. AI generation also creates complete workouts you can save and reuse.

### 11.3 Subscription Questions

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**
A: Yes. Cancel anytime through your app store settings. You'll retain access until the current billing period ends.

**Q: Will I lose my data if I cancel my subscription?**
A: No. Your workouts and profile data remain saved. You'll lose access to Pro features (AI generation), but Basic features remain available.

**Q: What happens if I don't renew my subscription?**
A: You'll revert to the Basic plan. All your data remains, but Pro features become unavailable.

**Q: Can I switch between subscription plans?**
A: Yes, through your app store subscription settings. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time.

**Q: Do subscriptions include a free trial?**
A: Free trials may be offered periodically. Check the app for current offers.

**Q: Are subscriptions refundable?**
A: Refunds are subject to app store policies. Contact Apple Support (iOS) or Google Play Support (Android) for refund requests.

### 11.4 Health & Safety Questions

**Q: Is Workouch a substitute for a personal trainer?**
A: Workouch provides workout planning and tracking tools, but is not a substitute for professional fitness advice or medical guidance. Consult healthcare professionals for medical advice.

**Q: Can Workouch help me with injuries?**
A: You can specify injuries/limitations in "The Neat" mode. However, always consult healthcare professionals for injury-specific guidance. We are not liable for injuries resulting from workout use.

**Q: How accurate are BMI and TDEE calculations?**
A: These are estimates based on standard formulas and your profile data. Individual variations exist. Consult healthcare professionals for personalized medical or nutritional advice.

**Q: Is Workouch suitable for beginners?**
A: Yes! Workouch is designed for all fitness levels. Beginners can use AI-generated workouts tailored to their level, or manually create simple workouts.

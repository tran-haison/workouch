## Long Description (For App Stores - Up to 4000 characters)

Workouch is your ultimate AI workout partner, planner, generator, and fitness tracker, combining the convenience of AI-powered workout generation with the flexibility of manual workout creation. Whether you're a beginner seeking guidance or a fitness enthusiast craving variety, Workouch has everything you need to achieve your goals.

1. AI-POWERED WORKOUT BUILDER

"The Shuffle" Mode:
• Describe what you need in a single sentence, and wait for our AI to create a personalized workout in seconds. Simply say "I want a full-body workout at home", then our advanced AI will analyze your preferences, with your profile data, combining with thousands of exercises to build the perfect routine for you.

"The Neat" Mode:
• For those who prefer detailed customization, "The Neat" mode gives you full control. Specify your workout name, duration, intensity level, fitness goals, target body parts, available equipments, location, and any injuries or limitations. Our AI uses these precise parameters to generate a workout that matches your exact specifications.

Browse Extensive Exercise Library with information including:

• Target muscles and body parts

• Step-by-step instructions

• and many more
